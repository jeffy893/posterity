## Creative Projects

### The End of Rom Com
A satirical short story set in the Peterson-Smith household — a polyamorous academic family navigating scheduling conflicts, passive-aggressive notes, and the relentless intellectualizing of feelings. Features:
- Professor Ashton Peterson, who frames every domestic failure as publishable material
- Professor Vivian Smith, whose patience has been eroded by a thousand student essays
- Chloe, a PhD in the Sociology of Reproductive Futures, trapped between biology and theory
- Dave, a systems manager from Hinge who thinks "kitchen table polyamory" means ordering pizza for three
- Sarah, a stand-up comedian who turns the whole mess into a Netflix special

> *"The rom-com was dead. Long live the peer-reviewed personal essay."*

## Health & Wellness

### Pre-BizTrip Health Plan
A structured approach to nutrition, activity, and rest:

**Schedule:**
- 5:00 AM wake / 9:00 PM bedtime / 2:00 PM siesta
- 3 meals per day using the plate method

**Plate Composition:**
- ~1/4 base (quinoa, beans)
- ~1/4 protein (beef liver, salmon, steak, pollo asada)
- ~1/3 vegetables (stir-fry: carrots, onions, broccoli, red pepper, zucchini, garlic)
- Healthy fats (goat butter), probiotics (achar), seasonal fruit

**Exercise:** Pickleball (Mondays), swimming and walking (twice weekly)

**Mental Habits:** Daily reading and writing

### Homie
A character study exploring self-sufficiency — a computer engineer and homesteader in Arizona who raises cows, sheep, and chickens, grows vegetables in a greenhouse, consults the Foxfire journals, and finds deep satisfaction in producing rather than consuming.
