## Jefferson Richards — In a Nutshell (1993–2023)

A biographical timeline covering:

- **Early Life** — Born on a Blue Moon (August 30th), grew up in a boating lifestyle on Bronco Rd outside Detroit, MI. Explored nature in Carl's Woods with cousin Sean Wilson.
- **Family Heritage** — Great-Grandfather Hursley wrote the soap opera *General Hospital*. Grandfather Richards worked with Cesar Chavez as a lawyer and served as Yuma District Attorney.
- **Education** — UC Davis (Sustainable Agriculture & Statistics), San Diego Mesa College (Associate's in Mathematics), University of Arizona (Mathematics & Finance).
- **Kentucky** — Worked on the family tobacco farm, learned subsistence living, wrote and received love letters.
- **Apprenticeship** — Learned PLM and software architecture from his father Paul Richards, who had 35+ years of experience from EDS automotive → DoD → NASA. Developed an NLP algorithm to extract the "DNA sequence" of poetry and constitutions.
- **Career** — Co-founded Sensutec (NLP/AI), actuarial internship at Nautilus, led the Risk Runners club at U of A, worked at Red Fox Analytics.
- **Wisdom from Elders** — Shadowed Grandfather Richards (legal wisdom), supported Grandmother Hursley (peace), learned grace from Gramma Richards.
