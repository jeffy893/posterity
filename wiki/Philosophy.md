## Dunbar Theory & Social Dynamics

### Dunbar Calculus of Value
A mathematical framework for understanding human social capacity and value creation. Extends Robin Dunbar's research on the cognitive limit (~150 people) to model how value flows through constrained social networks.

### Dunbar Bowls Dissertation Outline
An academic framework proposing that we live in metaphorical "bowls," not bubbles. Key arguments:
- Communities are limited by Dunbar's Number (~150 relationships)
- Every celebrity admitted into our attention displaces someone who may be in need
- Institutional friction (police, schools, church) determines how easily one can venture to the edge of their social bowl
- Central thought experiment: Conservation of Momentum applied to social dynamics — walking on a perfectly round bowl on ice causes the bowl to curve up behind you

### Dignity on Both Sides
A conflict resolution framework built on the premise: **"Not all esteem is created equal, but all dignity is."**

Key concepts:
- **Solidarity Index** — entering a new "3rd space" is like shorting a solidarity index
- **Dignity as a shorted call option** — the right to buy at an ideally lower price
- **Esteem as a shorted put option** — the right to sell at an ideally higher price
- **Morale Model of Infectious Ideas** — change is proportional to transactions, probability of infection, and the ratio of infected to uninfected participants
- Draws on Schrödinger's logic, insurance governance (TRIA), and the Owner-Manager Dichotomy

## Behavioral Economics & Psychology

### Prospect Theory of Mating and Dating
Application of Kahneman & Tversky's Prospect Theory to human relationships and decision-making in romantic contexts.

### Queen's Gambit of Go
A strategic analysis built on the assumption that "90% of billionaires are the first child of at least 4 children in a nuclear family." Explores:
- The Fault of Human Truth (FHT) — believing something is good for "them" but not "them over there"
- Conway's Law, Parkinson's Law, and Murphy's Law as boundaries
- Psychological states: Anima ↔ Animus, Survitalism ↔ Narcissism ↔ Schizophrenia
- Austrian Economics and the value of a glass of water
- Bloom's Taxonomy as horizontal and vertical forces

### Juice Worth the Squeeze
A compendium of ideas accrued over years, framed as "names of concept" awaiting proof of concept. Includes:

| Concept | Core Idea |
|---|---|
| Survitality | Survival and vitality of the synapse (flow of resources) |
| Courageous Fidelian | Fidelity pities Patience as Courage pities Confidence |
| Schrödinger's Fish | Impeachment disrupts the economy |
| Street Sweeping on Roller Skates | Decay is swept away by social services |
| Medical Malpractice Precedence | Idiosyncrasy Credit and adverse reactions to influencers |
| Service Science: Volumetrics | PV=nRT applied to social dynamics |
| Service Science: Dam Theory | Batching like Colorado River recreation |
| Family FHIR | Elastic Stack as longitudinal store for bottom-up wellness |

## Creative Philosophy

### Gracers
A screenplay about four athletes (gymnast, figure skater, diver, basketball player) pursuing perfection in grace. They perform their most graceful moves and die immediately after — except the diver, who survives and must reckon with what they were all chasing. A meditation on the pursuit of perfection vs. finding meaning in the everyday.

### West African Romeo and Juliet ("Savannah's Star-Crossed Souls")
A cross-cultural adaptation set in fictional West African tribes ("Sunset" and "Moonrise"), exploring love, religious conversion, tribal conflict, and universal themes of unity.

### Tired of Vanity
A synthesis of cognitive bias research, Vedic philosophy, the Book of Ecclesiastes, and Hermetic principles. Explores:
- Affective forecasting errors and present bias
- Maya (illusion) and Parinama (impermanence)
- The Hermetic principles of Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, and Mentalism
- Finding acceptance in the cyclical nature of energy and experience

### Hermes
A concept for a celestial messaging app that uses star positioning to host messages in the sky, accessible to anyone tuned to the right channel.

### Homie
A character study of a self-sufficient computer engineer and homesteader in Arizona who has everything he needs but remains "a step removed from what he didn't quite understand" about human connection.

## My Father and AI (1995)
A tribute comparing Larry Ellison (Oracle) to Marcus Aurelius and Paul Richards (the author's father, at EDS in Detroit) to Epictetus. Paul pioneered Object-Oriented Technology and agentic AI in the automotive industry 30 years ago — building autonomous decision-making systems before the world had a name for them.

> *"The Emperor owned the Oracle. But the Slave mastered the Java, and in doing so, he set the future free."*
