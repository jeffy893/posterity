# Core Themes

The threads that run through the entire Posterity collection.

---

## 1. Human Social Capacity

Grounded in Robin Dunbar's research, these works explore the cognitive limits of human relationships (~150 people) and build mathematical frameworks for understanding how value flows through constrained social networks.

Key works: *Dunbar Calculus of Value*, *Dunbar Bowls Dissertation Outline*, *Dignity on Both Sides*

---

## 2. Constitutional Principles

Analysis of freedom of assembly, speech, and religion in contemporary context. The central question — "Will we be in a place where the freedom to assemble will be challenged?" — drives much of the philosophical inquiry.

Key works: *Common Grievances*, *Bylaws on Indemnifications*, *Testimony About Current Leadership*

---

## 3. Behavioral Economics

Application of economic models (Prospect Theory, Austrian Economics, game theory) to human relationships, decision-making, and social dynamics. The recurring metaphor of "3sens" — does it make cents, sense, and scents?

Key works: *Prospect Theory of Mating and Dating*, *Queen's Gambit of Go*, *Juice Worth the Squeeze*

---

## 4. Cultural Adaptation

Cross-cultural understanding explored through creative works, family heritage, and personal experience spanning Detroit, California, Kentucky, Arizona, and Bermuda. Universal human experiences examined through diverse lenses.

Key works: *West African Romeo and Juliet*, *Letters From America*, *Letters from Tucson*, *Nashim Frame of Reference*

---

## 5. Authentic Living

A persistent critique of superficiality and exploration of meaningful existence. The tension between vanity and substance, between consuming and producing, between performing and being.

Key works: *Tired of Vanity*, *Homie*, *Hermes*, *Gracers*

---

## 6. Separation of Church, State, and Entertainment

Extending John Locke's framework to include entertainment as a third domain:
- **Church** → Long-term happiness (spiritual fulfillment)
- **State** → Short-term happiness (civic order)
- **Entertainment** → Instantaneous happiness (cultural distraction)

The works examine how these domains interact and what happens when their boundaries blur.

---

## 7. Legacy and Posterity

The overarching meta-theme: preserving human thought in an age of AI-generated noise. These writings exist as a signal amid the saturation — a record of what it means to be human during a period of institutional pressure and cultural transformation.

> *"AI-driven content saturation makes it impossible for human creators to be seen, massively devaluing their work. The future of AI isn't just generating more noise, but helping us find the signal of what truly matters."*
