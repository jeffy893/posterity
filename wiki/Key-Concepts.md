# Key Concepts

A glossary of the original theoretical concepts developed across the Posterity collection.

---

## Dunbar Bowls
We live in metaphorical "bowls," not bubbles. Our social community is limited to ~150 people (Dunbar's Number), and every celebrity or distant figure we admit into our attention displaces someone nearby who may be in need. The friction between our bowl and the broader fabric of solidarity determines how easily we can venture to the edge. Institutions (police, schools, church) create friction that makes traversal difficult.

**Thought experiment:** Walking on a perfectly round bowl on a sheet of ice — Conservation of Momentum causes the bowl to curve up behind whatever direction you're headed.

---

## Solidarity Index
A financial metaphor for social cohesion. Entering a new "3rd space" (beyond home and work) is like shorting a solidarity index — you borrow shares of solidarity and sell them at the current price, banking on the cost going down so you can buy back cheaper. The index tends toward the impetus of responsibility between the dignity and esteem of the community's micro-hegemon.

---

## Dignity vs. Esteem
- **Dignity** = a shorted call option (the right to buy at an ideally lower price). All dignity is created equal.
- **Esteem** = a shorted put option (the right to sell at an ideally higher price). Not all esteem is created equal — not all esteem has a strong foundation.

---

## 3sens
A decision-making heuristic: Does it make **cents** (financial), **sense** (logical), and **scents** (intuitive/aesthetic)? Used as a filter for evaluating ideas and actions.

---

## Survitality
"Survival and Vitality of the synapse" — the flow of resources through a system. A framework for understanding how systems maintain themselves through the balance of survival needs and vital energy. Predates public awareness of AI concepts.

---

## Morale Model of Infectious Ideas
A mathematical model where the change in those who "catch" logical viruses is proportional to:
- Number of transactions
- Probability of infection
- Number of uninfected transactors × number of infected transactors

And inversely proportional to the total number of transactors. "Active" is measured by the likelihood of fighting if left alone on the battlefield. "Passive" can become active depending on company.

---

## The Fault of Human Truth (FHT)
The cognitive error of believing something is good for "them" but not "them over there." Those who believe themselves to be educated and in-group (the "W" quadrant) are most likely to commit this fault.

---

## Service Science: Volumetrics
Applying the ideal gas law (PV=nRT) to social dynamics:
- n (people) down → T (temperature/tension) up
- V (volume/space) down → T down
- Insulation through family relationship management

---

## Service Science: Dam Theory
Social engagement modeled as fluid dynamics — batching interactions like Colorado River recreation management. Incorporates gradient engagement entropy, Conway's Law, Parkinson's Law, and fluid queue theory.

---

## Family FHIR
Using the Elastic Stack as a longitudinal data store for bottom-up family wellness. The "Modern Family" medic as care coordinator — like a platoon medic who first asks about sleep, food, exercise, and water before reaching for medication.

---

## Material, Social, and Scope Capital
Money alone doesn't capture modern value. Three forms of capital:
- **Material** — traditional financial/physical resources (skewed right)
- **Social** — relationships and network value (skewed left)
- **Scope** — breadth of influence and reach (normally distributed)

---

## The Modern Noble Lie
Iniquity manifests in aesthetic appearance (facial features), not in labor inequality (as seen in the hands). Beauty disrupts priorities, but objectivity lies in the socialization of taste (Kant's *Critique of Judgment*).

---

## Courageous Fidelian
"Fidelity pities Patience as Courage pities Confidence." Allegiance to process over outcome — the rifle vs. the shotgun approach.
